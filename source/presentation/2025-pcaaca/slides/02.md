## Lacan's Three Registers

**The Imaginary:**  
illusion but not pure fiction

**The Symbolic:**  
language and social constructs

**The Real:**  
actually existing and true

Notes:

Freud had the ideal ego, ego-ideal, superego -- Lacan had the imaginary, the symbolic, the real

- imaginary: illusion but not pure fiction, what someone perceives as normal, conscious thought
- symbolic: language and social constructs, unconscious thought, the big Other
- real: imaginary and symbolic are reality, which is opposed to the Real -- actually existing and true, overwhelming and horrifying

## Oculus's Three Registers

**The Imaginary:**  
Kaylie and Tim's untrusted experiences

**The Symbolic:**  
the Lassar Glass and its reflections

**The Real:**  
the objective account of events from video cameras

Notes:

- everything that Kaylie and Tim experience is part of reality, but it is not The Real -- it is what they are perceiving based on the mirror's influence
- the Lassar Glass manipulates people around it and shows them a reflection of their worst fears and traits, so it starts to become reality for them
- Kaylie explained that the tapes would be an objective record of the events happening in the office, so everything that's on camera -- the video tapes, the computer monitors, the phone camera -- is The Real, which can be completely overwhelming to understand
