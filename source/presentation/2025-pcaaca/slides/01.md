Notes:

Oculus is a remake/retelling of Flanagan's own short film, Oculus: Chapter 3 -- the Man with the Plan. Takes place in one small room with one man trying to take down an evil mirror responsible for many deaths over the years. To make it a full-length feature, Flanagan settled on a story told through 2 timelines, Then and Now. Eventually as the film goes on, the two timelines move more quickly back and forth, sometimes overlapping.

---

<!-- .slide: data-background-image="./images/kaylie-tim-then.png" -->

Notes:

Then: two siblings Kaylie and Tim, as they struggle with family trauma they believe is caused by a supernatural antique mirror their father bought for his office in their new home. Their father Alan begins to exhibit abusive behavior, especially toward their mother Marie, who becomes almost feral after Alan chains her by her neck to their bedroom wall. The strange occurances culminate with Alan killing Marie before pulling the trigger on himself as Tim hold the gun. Time is subsequently committed to a juvenile mental facility.

---

<!-- .slide: data-background-image="./images/kaylie-tim-now.jpg" -->

Notes:

Now: Kaylie and Tim reunite 11 years later, after Tim is released from the psychiatric hospital. He has been conditioned to believe there was no supernatural power in the family's antique mirror, and that his parents' death about a decade before was a murder-suicide. Kaylie begs him to remember the promise they made, that when they were big and stong, they would destroy the mirror and defeat its ghostly evil.
